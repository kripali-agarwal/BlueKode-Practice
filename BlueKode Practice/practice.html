About Me:

Kripali Agarwal is a 16-year-old student driven by curiosity and a belief that growth begins at the edge of what is already known.

She is drawn to questions that challenge boundaries—of knowledge, perspective, and possibility. To Kripali, stepping beyond what is familiar is not about having all the answers, but about being willing to question, learn, and look at things from a different perspective.

She believes that boundaries are rarely permanent. They can be challenged, expanded, and sometimes redefined entirely. This shapes the way she approaches learning: with curiosity, openness to unfamiliar ideas, and a desire to understand things more deeply rather than simply accept them as they are.

Her interests lie across STEM, leadership, communication, and ideas that can create meaningful change. Rather than following a predetermined path, Kripali hopes to keep exploring where curiosity takes her, while using what she learns to create opportunities for herself and others.

For her, pushing boundaries is not about crossing them simply for the sake of it. It is about discovering what lies beyond them—and asking what else might be possible.
